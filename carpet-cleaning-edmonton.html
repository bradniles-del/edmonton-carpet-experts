FAQs

Carpet Cleaning Questions — Edmonton Homeowners Ask

How long does carpet cleaning take?

A typical room takes 20–45 minutes depending on size and condition. A full home can take 2–4 hours. We'll give you a more accurate estimate when you book.

How long until the carpet is dry?

Drying time is typically 4–8 hours depending on airflow, humidity, and carpet thickness. Opening windows and running fans speeds up drying significantly.

Do I need to move furniture before you arrive?

If possible, clearing smaller items off the carpet helps us work more efficiently. We can work around larger furniture pieces or discuss moving options when you book.

Can you remove old or set-in stains?

Many older stains can be significantly reduced or removed with professional treatment. Results depend on the stain type, how long it's been there, and the carpet fibre — we'll be upfront with you about what to expect.

How often should I have my carpets professionally cleaned?

Most homes benefit from professional cleaning every 12–18 months. Homes with pets or children may benefit from cleaning every 6–12 months.

Do you serve areas outside Edmonton?

Yes — we serve Sherwood Park, St. Albert, Beaumont, Leduc, Spruce Grove, and other communities in the greater Edmonton area. See our service areas page for the full list.
