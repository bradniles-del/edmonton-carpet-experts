Carpet Repair Services We Offer

We handle the most common carpet repair issues Edmonton homeowners deal with — from rippling and buckling to seam damage and localized repairs.

Carpet Stretching

Ripples and buckles are a tripping hazard and look bad. We re-stretch loose carpet back tight to the tack strips, restoring a smooth, flat surface throughout the room.

✂️

Seam Repair

Carpet seams can separate over time, especially in high-traffic areas. We repair split or frayed seams so they lay flat and are virtually invisible.

🔲

Patch Repair

Burned areas, severe stains, or localized damage can often be patched using matching carpet. A well-done patch is far more cost-effective than replacing the whole floor.

🚪

Doorway & Transition Repair

Carpet at doorways and transitions between rooms often takes extra wear. We re-tuck, trim, and secure carpet edges for a clean, safe finish.

📐

Re-Installation

Carpet that has come completely away from tack strips or shifted significantly can be properly re-installed rather than replaced — often at a fraction of the cost.

Is Repair Right for You?

Signs Your Carpet Needs Repair

These are common issues that can often be repaired rather than replaced.

Ripples or Buckling

Waves or bumps across the carpet surface are usually a stretching issue and can be corrected without replacement.

Visible Seam Gaps

If you can see where two carpet sections meet or they've started to separate, a seam repair can make them nearly invisible again.

Fraying at Edges or Doorways

Carpet edges that are fraying or unravelling can be trimmed and re-secured before the damage spreads further.

Burn Marks or Isolated Damage

Small burned or badly damaged areas are good candidates for patching — especially if you have leftover carpet from installation.

FAQs

Carpet Repair Questions

Is carpet repair worth it, or should I just replace?

In many cases, repair is absolutely worth it — especially for stretching, seam issues, or localized damage. If the overall carpet is in good condition but has specific problem areas, repair is usually the smarter financial choice. We'll give you an honest assessment.

Why does carpet develop ripples and buckles?

Carpet rippling is common in Edmonton homes and can happen due to improper installation, humidity changes, heavy furniture being dragged across the carpet, or simply age and use. It's a fixable problem in most cases.

Do I need to have leftover carpet for a patch repair?

Leftover carpet from the original installation makes for the best match, but it's not always necessary. We can discuss options based on your specific situation.

How long does carpet repair take?

Most repairs — including stretching a full room — can be completed in 1–3 hours. More complex work may take longer. We'll give you an estimated time when we assess the job.

Can you repair carpet and clean it on the same visit?

In many cases, yes. Let us know when you request a quote and we'll coordinate both services efficiently.
